About GooseGames

A playful and polished fake game marketplace built to feel like Amazon.

We’re all about the fun

GooseGames is a school assessment project with a joyful storefront design. The experience includes search, filters, deals, cart interactions, and a checkout flow that looks official without processing real payments.

What you’ll find here

  • Lots of polished game cards with animated sale tags
  • A cart drawer that expands from the side
  • Search and category pages
  • Theme switching between light and dark modes
  • Checkout simulation with fake payment validation

Why this feels real

The page structure is built so every section is separate, and the shopping flow is smooth. There are large sale banners, limited-time tags, and a sidebar cart experience to make the storefront feel trustworthy and immersive.
